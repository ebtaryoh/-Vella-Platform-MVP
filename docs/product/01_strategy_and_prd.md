## 2. Market Research (As of Sept 2026)
*   **Competitor Landscape:** Uber exited Nigeria in Sept 2026 due to harsh economic conditions and regulatory pressure. Bolt and inDrive lead the market, with local players like LagRide and Rida gaining share.
*   **Driver Economics:** Fuel (PMS) sits around ₦1,350/liter, destroying driver margins. Platform commissions (15-25%) are heavily protested. Drivers frequently demand offline trips to negotiate better fares.
*   **Safety:** Severe concerns remain regarding kidnapping, robberies, and offline rides.
*   **Passenger Experience:** High prices and unpredictable service (drivers asking for extra cash, refusing trips). 
*   **Corporate/Event Market:** Highly fragmented. Companies rely on specialized fleets (e.g., Drivecare, Femadec) for events and corporate shuttles, with limited tech integration.

## 3. SWOT Analysis
*   **Strengths (Proposed):** Transparent driver economics, Trusted Driver relationships, native B2B/Event modules, safety-first architecture.
*   **Weaknesses:** New market entrant; capital intensive to acquire initial liquidity (drivers/riders).
*   **Opportunities:** Uber's recent exit creates a vacuum. Drivers are desperate for a fairer commission/economic model. Huge untapped B2B/Corporate event tech market.
*   **Threats:** Fuel price volatility, regulatory shifts, entrenched duopoly (Bolt/inDrive).

## 4. User Personas
1.  **The Daily Commuter (Chika, 28):** Needs reliable, safe transit to work. Values price transparency and knowing her driver.
2.  **The Hustling Driver (Kunle, 35):** Needs to make a profit despite ₦1,350/liter fuel. Needs to know if a trip is worth the fuel.
3.  **The Corporate Admin (Tola, 42):** Needs to manage staff transport and airport transfers with consolidated invoicing.
4.  **The Event Organizer (Emeka, 30):** Needs to coordinate buses from 5 Lagos locations for a 2,000-person church convention.

## 5. MVP Scope & Feature Prioritization
**Phase 1: Foundation (Must Have)**
*   User & Driver Authentication (Strict Verification workflow).
*   Core Ride Engine (Immediate & Scheduled Rides).
*   Smart Pricing & Basic Negotiation.
*   Driver Economics Engine (Transparency on earnings vs. estimated fuel).
*   Trusted Driver System (Core Differentiator).
*   Safety System (SOS, Trip Sharing).
*   Payments (Wallet, Card, Cash).
*   Admin Command Center.
*   **"AC Guaranteed" & Ride Preferences:** Climate comfort pricing integration.
*   **"No-Change" Wallet Escrow:** Auto-transfer change from passenger to driver wallet.
*   **Vella SecureAudio:** Opt-in encrypted audio recording for safety disputes.

**Phase 2: Expansion (Should Have)**
*   Family Accounts.
*   Business Mobility (Corporate dashboard).
*   Event Transportation Module.
*   **Vella SafeSister:** Women+ Connect prioritizing female driver/passenger matches.
*   **Vella Aviation:** Scheduled airport concierge with flight tracking API.
*   **Vella Guild:** Gamified driver loyalty tiers (HMO/Maintenance benefits).

**Phase 3: Ecosystem (Later)**
*   Fleet Management.
*   Delivery / Intercity.
*   Advanced AI Forecasting.

## 6. Development Roadmap
*   **Phase 0:** Research, Architecture, Design System (Current).
*   **Phase 1:** Core Backend, DB, API Gateway.
*   **Phase 2:** Admin Dashboard (Command Center & Driver Verification).
*   **Phase 3:** Driver Mobile App (Economics focused).
*   **Phase 4:** Passenger Mobile App (Trust/Safety focused).
*   **Phase 5:** Payment & Wallet Integration.
*   **Phase 6:** Testing, QA, and Beta Launch in Lagos (focusing on Lekki, VI, Ikeja corridors).
